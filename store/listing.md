# ASTRAL

Short description: Bend light, split the spectrum, and solve 101 cosmic optical puzzles.

Guide a single ray through a quiet universe of glass, color, and curved space.

Place a limited set of prisms, mirrors, lenses, and green filters. Split white light into a spectrum and guide green, blue, red, and yellow to their matching receivers. Later chambers introduce narrow passages, shielded targets, and tighter wavelength matching.

Explore 101 chambers. Every tenth level enters a dimension where light curves through space. Every fifth completed chamber reveals a fact about the cosmos.

Includes local saves, optional interaction sounds, hints, and offline play in the Android edition.
