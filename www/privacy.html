Privacy

Updated 28 September 2026

ASTRAL stores game progress and sound preferences on your device. The current game includes no account system, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or in-game purchases. Game progress is not sent to a game server.

When you play the web version, the hosting provider processes ordinary web requests, which can include your IP address and browser information. Links to NASA open an external site with its own privacy practices. Google Play may process download and diagnostic information under Google's policies.

Clearing this site's storage, or clearing the Android app's data, removes local progress. Uninstalling the Android app removes its local data, subject to any device backup behavior.

Publication draft: Developer identity and support contact must be added before submitting this policy to Google Play.
